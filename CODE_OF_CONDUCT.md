# Code of Conduct

This repository is DoCoNEXT's public issue tracker for `@doconext/nc-ui`. It
does not take code contributions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), so
what it hosts is bug reports, feature requests, and the discussion around them.
This page says what we expect there, and what happens when that is not met.

## What we expect

- **Be precise, and be kind.** A report that says what you did, what happened
  and what you expected is the most useful thing you can give us. Writing it
  politely costs nothing.
- **Argue about the software, not the person.** Disagreement about a design is
  welcome. Contempt is not.
- **Respect people's time.** Do not reopen a closed issue to restate it, do
  not pull maintainers into unrelated threads, and do not demand a timeline.
  We would rather tell you honestly how long something will take than promise
  a date (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- **Keep other people's data out of it.** Screenshots of an app show whatever
  was on the screen, and this tracker is public. Remove names, file names,
  identifiers and figures before posting them.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language, publishing
someone's private information, threats, and sustained disruption of a
discussion. This applies to everyone here, the maintainers included.

## Where this applies

Everything under this repository: issues and their comments, and security
reports sent through the Security tab or to the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or delete a comment, lock a thread, close an issue,
or block an account from the repository. Where the behaviour is clear, they
may do so without warning. We will say why.

To report someone's behaviour, or to contest a decision taken under this page,
write to **support@doconext.com**. A person reads every report, keeps it
confidential, and answers it.
